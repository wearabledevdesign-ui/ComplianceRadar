Architecture Overview

ComplianceRadar uses a Hybrid Direct-Connect architecture, bypassing third-party aggregators to access government servers directly across 7 jurisdictions.

The 8-Source Intelligence Pipeline

🇨🇭 Switzerland: Fedlex

Deep SPARQL semantic queries retrieve primary legislation from the Federal Law database.

🇨🇭 Switzerland: FINMA

Official "Delivery Solution" RSS feed monitors circulars, guidance, and announcements from the Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority.

🇪🇺 European Union: EUR-Lex

High-volume sync via the EU Publications Office "Cellar" endpoint, optimized with Newest-First sort to ensure recent 2026 regulations are never missed.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom: UK Lex

Automated ingestion of the UK National Archives Atom feed, supporting both primary acts and statutory instruments.

🇺🇸 United States: Federal Register

JSON API integration tracking "Final Rules" published by every US agency, from the SEC to the EPA.

🇮🇹 Italy: Parliament & Gazette

Dual-feed system combining deep historical SPARQL data with real-time RSS monitoring of the Gazzetta Ufficiale.

🇫🇷 France: National Archives

Optimized ingestion of French legislative feeds, ensuring native-language accuracy before AI translation.

🇩🇪 Germany: National Archives

Optimized ingestion of German legislative feeds, ensuring native-language accuracy before AI translation.

Granular Logic & Filtering

Rapid triage of large data volumes through specialized logic engines designed for professional workflows.

📅 "New Today" Engine

A persistent monitoring system based on Calendar Day Logic. Unlike standard apps that reset highlights on refresh, highlighting does not reset—instead, it accumulates every update detected since 00:00:00 of the current day.

  • Baseline calculates "Today Start" at midnight
  • Blue Dot indicator persists throughout the day
  • Multi-refresh support—accumulates without clearing
  • Single-tap "New Only" filter access

📌 Sticky Regulations

Grant critical items Immortal Status. Pinned regulations are strictly excluded from the automated cleanup logic, remaining in the dashboard indefinitely until unpinned.

  • Immune to retention window archival
  • Dedicated "Pinned Only" dashboard view
  • Persistent cross-sync storage
  • Push Pin icon grants immunity

🔍 Advanced Search

Global search bar indexes three data layers simultaneously for comprehensive discovery.

  • Primary Title (English or translated)
  • Technical Description & abstract
  • Taxonomy Labels & categories

📆 "Upcoming" Shortcut

Automatic high-density view for immediate priority management.

  • Filters for deadlines within next 7 calendar days
  • Effective dates highlighted in chronological order
  • Quick triage for compliance requirements

🤖 On-Device AI Core

High-quality translation with absolute privacy. Every native regulation is transformed into professional English using Local ML Kit Processing.

Customizable Retention Policy

In Refresh Settings, define the dashboard's historical depth. During every sync, a background "Purge Engine" surgically deletes records older than your selected window, ensuring lightweight performance:

  • 1 Month — High-velocity regulatory tracking
  • 3 Months — Quarterly compliance cycles
  • 6 Months — Standard (default) retention window
  • 1 Year — Extended historical archive

Technical Specifications

Data Pipeline Direct 8-source integration: Authoritative government streams only. Zero third-party aggregators.
History Window Configurable retention: 1 month, 3 months, 6 months (standard), or 12 months.
Translation Engine Google ML Kit on-device processing. Dynamic re-translation on source updates. Zero cloud data transmission.
Notification Logic Pro-active "Watchlist" system. System-level alerts triggered during background maintenance cycles.
Refresh Scheduling User-defined target sync time (e.g., 08:30 AM). Smart delay calculation for fresh workday start.
Connectivity Toggle for cellular data usage. Wi-Fi-only mode prevents carrier charges during background tasks.
Platform Optimized for Android 13+. Minimum SDK 26. Built with high-density Jetpack Compose UI.
Subscription Freemium model. Free Swiss/FINMA tier. Premium Global tier via Play Billing.
