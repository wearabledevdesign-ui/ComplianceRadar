Functional Deep Dive

Designed for users who require technical transparency and granular control over their regulatory data footprint.

📅 "New Today" Engine

A persistent monitoring system based on the Calendar Day. Highlighting does not reset on refresh; instead, it accumulates every update detected since 00:00:00 of the current day.

  • Accumulative multi-refresh support
  • Visual "Blue Dot" indicators
  • Single-tap "New Only" triage

📌 Sticky Regulations

Grant critical items Immunity Status. Pinned regulations are strictly excluded from the automated cleanup logic, remaining in the dashboard indefinitely until unpinned.

  • Immune to 180-day archival
  • Dedicated "Pinned Only" dashboard
  • Persistent cross-sync storage

🤖 On-Device AI Core

High-quality translation with absolute privacy. Every native regulation is transformed into professional English using Local ML Kit Processing.

  • Zero-cloud data leakage
  • Dynamic re-translation on source change
  • Unified "% EN" progress metrics
